Reviewed 2026-09-29: Sealed Waste Wise outreach totes delayed during active same-day delivery must not be opened in public, split into personal bags, sent by rideshare, or moved with personal spending. If ordinary transit strands an approved tote before the host setup window closes, the Public Works Tote Courier Desk may add a no-cost intercept only before 10:05 on the service day. Use subject line "WWA tote intercept" and include the tote label, current safe pickup point, destination event, and setup deadline. If no intercept is confirmed before the host setup window closes, use only materials already on site.

Stormwater-safe cleanup supplies: For hub cleanups at stencil or curb-line sites, do not load liquid degreasers, soaps, or citrus cleaners when a current stormwater notice says inlet grates are open or draining to a creek. Before the parks cart is sealed, replace those liquids with dry brushes, absorbent pads, and plain-water spray bottles if already available. Jamal Reed is the volunteer lead who can change the Willow Creek scrub tote until the stated sealing time. Hub volunteers should not buy substitute chemicals, interpret stormwater law, or transport sealed parks carts in personal vehicles.

Publicity-photo checklist adopted before the 2026 program season: For youth or all-ages park programs, volunteer materials sent for public promotion should avoid recognizable minors unless Lakehaven Parks Communications has confirmed releases in writing. Do not rely on old camp folders, volunteer recollection, or apparent friendliness of a group photo. Safe substitutes include close-up plants, habitat details, tools without faces, empty trails, or boardwalk scenes without identifiable visitors. Before a communications queue locks, a suspect image can be replaced through the routine package contact; after queue lock, volunteer-side changes cannot prevent first publication.

Dissolved-oxygen meter checkout checklist adopted before the 2026 pond-program season: DO-7 may be packed for volunteer pond-dip observation kits only after the city calibration station completes the pre-rinse and both standard readings. If the station stops with a CAL-SLEEVE lock during an active checkout, only Rowan Bell may release the staff-controlled calibration sleeve; volunteers must not use personal meters, reset the station, or bypass the lock. A same-day sleeve release must include the DO-7 asset tag and the pond-dip kit activity label before 11:10 on packing day. If calibration is not completed before the kit courier seals the bins, omit the dissolved-oxygen forms from that program kit.

Standing reference: For optional Harborview Bus Agency accessibility-dashboard digests, use only public, nonconfidential stop-condition observations. Minimum for a same-cycle digest is six distinct stop observations; with fewer than six, hold notes for the ordinary monthly advisory summary. Allowed category labels: seating_or_shelter, curb_or_boarding_area, sign_or_schedule_visibility, lighting, sidewalk_obstruction. Send draftable CSV-style text to accessibility.dashboard@harborviewbus.org with subject 'May accessibility digest - public observations'. Do not include rider names, accounts, fare eligibility, medical details, or service-outcome demands.

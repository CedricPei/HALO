Dishwasher delay-start note
The Westlake DW-420 delay button postpones the beginning of a cycle in one-hour steps. After selecting the normal cycle, press Delay until the display shows the desired number of hours, then press Start and close the door. The dishwasher remains off until the delayed start time.

Court observation badge failure protocol, saved 2026-01-02: If a supervised observation attempt has already cleared security and a visitor badge kiosk shows HELD-IN-PROGRESS before courtroom entry, only the Court Education Desk may clear or reissue the record. Security officers cannot override entry scanners, and students must not create a duplicate visitor pass. Use the messaging thread with Court Education Desk. It is permissible to disclose student full name, preclearance email subject, kiosk ID, visitor transaction/pass ID, and assigned courtroom. Ask for badge clearance before observer admission closes at docket call; after closure the attempt cannot be logged for that sitting.

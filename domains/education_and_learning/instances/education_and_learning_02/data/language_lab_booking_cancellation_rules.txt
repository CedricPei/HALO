Language lab booking cancellation rules, saved 2026-01-07: Optional one-on-one mock-drill bookings may be cancelled by the student through the existing message thread with Language Lab Front Desk. Include the booking code, appointment time, and reason if it is a certificate-required conflict. Do not ask an individual tutor to remove attendance. A cancellation prevents the lab no-show mark and $25 processing charge only if it is received before the roster export time shown in the lab roster notice. When a certificate-required screening assignment overlaps an optional mock drill and no later make-up is offered, cancellation is preferred over keeping the optional booking.

Effective 2026-01-02 for Virelli Flying Club learner flights: If a preflight checklist has already been opened for a scheduled dual lesson and a red-limit aircraft condition locks the checklist before engine start, the learner must not continue the checklist, troubleshoot the aircraft, or request a different aircraft independently. The allowed low-commitment escalation is a concise discrepancy notice to the assigned CFI and the Maintenance Duty Desk using only the tail number, checklist packet ID, discrepancy code, location, and scheduled engine-start hold. Maintenance or the CFI may clear, annotate, or transfer the lesson only before the hold expires.

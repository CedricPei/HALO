VFC Redbird simulator lockout protocol, Standing reference: If a learner Redbird session has already launched for a scheduled lesson and a hardware calibration lock disables student controls before the module can continue, the learner must not reboot the bay, move to another bay independently, edit the session record, or claim completion. The allowed low-commitment escalation is a concise notice to the verified VFC Simulator Duty Desk channel redbird-desk@virellifc.example, with the assigned CFI copied if known. Include only learner name, bay, Redbird session ID, module name, lockout code, and displayed hold deadline. The desk may clear the lock or authorize a same-block transfer only before the displayed hold expires; later desk tickets apply to future scheduling and are not retroactive for the expired simulator block.

VFC IACRA/FTN registrar intake policy, effective 2026-08-12: Learners may use the Training Records Registrar channel only after a CFI-confirmed pre-solo paperwork review is assigned. The low-commitment intake request may include learner name, review date/time, and whether an FTN confirmation is missing. Do not include medical details, test scores, logbook assertions, solo requests, or endorsement requests. Same-day registrar postings from the 14:30 intake are entered before the 17:00 roster export; later verified intakes post after the next business-day export and are not retroactive for earlier review rosters.

VFC simulator telemetry archive rules, Standing reference: Untagged Redbird session telemetry is retained only for same-day desk intake. Learners may request a low-commitment archive only after the assigned CFI selects a completed trace for a training packet or stage-check evidence review. Use the verified VFC Simulator Desk channel and include only learner name, Redbird session ID, module name, bay, run end time, and assigned CFI. Do not edit traces, claim logbook credit, or ask the desk to endorse performance. Same-day archive intake closes at 16:00 local time; after manifest closure, unarchived untagged runs remain visible only until overnight cleanup and are not added retroactively.


Winter quilt laundry reference: Nicolette's cotton-filled queen quilt requires a front-loading drum rated at least9kg so it can turn freely, on the label's gentle cold-water cycle. The basement South washer is6kg; the courtyard West washer is10kg and front-loading. Do not force the quilt into a smaller drum or substitute the center-agitator machine. SpinCycle permits a no-charge move of one unused prepaid wash to a compatible free machine through laundryhelp@spincycle.example with the existing card ID and receipt; no extra top-up, auto-reload, replacement card, or new subscription is authorized.

Use the kiln-vent contractor for a pre-firing draft check rather than altering exterior vent parts.

Studio dust cleanup standing guidance:
Light bench powder after hand sanding with fans off can be handled by closing the studio door, damp-wiping the bench, and wet-mopping the immediate work area before visitors. If any fan runs while sanding residue is still visible or the particulate monitor shows a clay-dust spike before a public visit, do not dry sweep or rely on ordinary airing-out. Keep the studio closed and use a clay-studio HEPA vacuum plus wet-wipe cleaning route before visitors enter.

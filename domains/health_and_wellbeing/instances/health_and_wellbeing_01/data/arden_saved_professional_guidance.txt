Physical therapy equipment note, updated 2026-02-18: For community walks longer than 75 minutes, start with one clean dry ankle-brace liner and carry a second fully dry clean liner. Do not rely on a damp liner for a long outdoor session. A hand-washed liner needs at least 18 hours of indoor air-drying before use. Replacement liners usually take three business days to arrive.

If a leg-lock button is missing or a leg slides during assembly, stop setup and do not substitute tape, towels, or improvised wedges. For same-day apartment assistance with bath-safety equipment, use Cascade Court Maintenance Desk before the 5:30 PM cutoff; keep any outreach as a draft for Arden to review if it involves scheduling help.
Primary care prep note, Standing reference: When Rose City Primary asks for a recent home blood-pressure log before a review, use five consecutive days of morning and evening readings if a cuff is available. Morning readings should be seated and before caffeine or exercise; evening readings should be before bedtime. For the July 21 9:30 AM review with an 8:00 AM upload cutoff, the latest full five-day sequence starts Thursday July 16 before any morning exercise. Do not buy a cuff solely for optional logging; borrowing a validated upper-arm cuff with fresh batteries is acceptable.
Nutrition coaching meal-prep note, Standing reference: For cooked batch meals, move shallow sealed portions into a working refrigerator within two hours of portioning. If working cold storage is unavailable after cooking has started, stop loading the failed refrigerator and arrange temporary refrigeration for the sealed portions rather than leaving them on the counter.

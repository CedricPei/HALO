Standing note: For apartment-lounge gatherings, do not place temporary side-door codes, unit numbers, or resident-only entry details in public event listings or email digests. If a listing needs arrival instructions, use: “meet in the front lobby and text Elias on arrival”; the lobby attendant can point guests to the lounge until 8:00 PM. A private note to the person managing the listing is appropriate before the digest edit cutoff; do not cancel the gathering, broaden the invitation, or ask others to manage building access.

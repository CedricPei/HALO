Mainland fluorescence carry-on kit note: the SeaGlow UV tile travels with two spare 160Wh lithium-ion battery packs and a printed UN38.3 summary. Island Pacific Air requires lithium-device preclearance before travel for spare packs over 100Wh; the request needs legal ID name, record locator, device model SeaGlow UV Tile SG-2, and battery watt-hours. Approval is the prerequisite for carrying the UV tile kit on the flight; posters and non-battery demo materials can travel without it.

HNL wet-demo cooler pre-check note: For interisland departures before 08:30 carrying sealed seawater or tidepool-water demo coolers larger than a personal item, use HNL Agriculture Inspection Desk's no-fee specialist review list when an interisland screening staffing advisory is posted. Request must be logged before 17:00 HST the previous calendar day with legal name, flight confirmation, cooler dimensions, contents, and departure time. Walk-up inspection remains possible, but during staffing advisories it commonly adds 35-50 minutes and does not protect tight shuttle or classroom setup buffers.

HNL baggage storage override note for outreach kits: service door is at the mauka end of Terminal 2 baggage claim near carousel 18. For latch jams, message or call the Duty Lead with bay number, storage confirmation, and ID name; kiosk agents cannot clear a jammed locker remotely.
MKK to Kaunakakai library transfer note: the ordinary airport bus normally reaches the library-area stop about 09:55 after the early HNL-MKK arrival. If a posted roadwork delay is 25 minutes or more and wet trays must be set up before attendees enter, use Akamai Molokai Taxi Dispatch for a low-commitment backup hold. Dispatch can place a no-charge backup vehicle hold by message until noon the day before pickup; include legal name, flight arrival time, library destination, and note that trunk space is needed for wet trays. Release the hold if the bus remains adequate.
LIH outreach courtesy cart note: for short LIH baggage-to-curb transfers with two or more rolling education crates and a hosted shuttle departing within 50 minutes of scheduled landing, the LIH Outreach Courtesy Cart Desk can place a no-fee courtesy cart reservation once the same-day arrival gate is posted. This is optional; regular baggage claim and curb access still work. Submit before the same-day signup cutoff with legal name, flight confirmation, event booth code, posted arrival gate, bag count, and shuttle departure time. Use carts@lihairport.example or +1-808-555-0126.

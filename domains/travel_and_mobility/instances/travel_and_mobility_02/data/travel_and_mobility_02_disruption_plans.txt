MBTA Access Sports Weekend companion-voucher note, saved 2026-12-15: For adaptive-sports events on the Lowell Line, the optional companion voucher is only worth requesting when Priya already has her own paid commuter-rail fare and a named teammate will ride the same train to help carry club goalball gear. Do not request for solo travel or before the outbound train is fixed. Requests go to accesssports@mbta.example and should include Priya's fare reference, event title, outbound train time, companion name, and the phrase 'low-vision rider with goalball equipment.' The voucher is a convenience benefit; standard fares and ordinary clinic check-in remain available without it.

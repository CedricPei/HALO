Ordinary ticket purchase or online trip changes do not create this request.

For first-time arrivals carrying tactile display boards, the optional Seaport Access dock-to-pavilion orientation walk is helpful; use mobilitydesk@seaportaccess.example during the posted volunteer-roster window, include event title, ferry arrival time, and 'low-vision visitor carrying tactile display boards.'

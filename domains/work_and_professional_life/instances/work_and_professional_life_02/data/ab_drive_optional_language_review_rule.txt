AB-DRIVE Optional Language Review Rule

For an upcoming AB-DRIVE session involving an employee whose AB-DRIVE qualification record is active, an observed terminology-comprehension difficulty may support one optional no-cost 30-minute Spanish terminology review of the approved participant handout. The workforce training coordinator may request the review from the Language Access Desk through the existing Language Access conversation.

The request must identify session ID AB-DRIVE-2026-10-30-EMP-447, course code AB-DRIVE, handout title AB-DRIVE Participant Handout, and the offered review window. Do not include the employee name, qualification status, attendance details, or other personal information. The review may clarify terminology only; it must not change assessment steps, qualification status, attendance, dispatch, or labor obligations. The reservation is optional and reversible until the editor begins.

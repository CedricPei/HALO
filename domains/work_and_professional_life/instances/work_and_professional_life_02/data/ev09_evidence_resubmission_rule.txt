EV-09 Evidence Resubmission Rule
Effective: 2026-06-01

For an approved internal evidence review sample, when the LMS Evidence Gateway halts a package solely because an attendance scan is submitted in an unsupported format, the workforce training coordinator may request one regenerated PDF from Records Support through the existing Evidence Corrections conversation. The request must identify the session ID, sample ID, course code, rejected filename, and review cutoff. Records Support can regenerate the file until 14:00 on the review date. The coordinator may not edit attendance evidence, convert or sign pages, or upload on an instructor's behalf. After the cutoff, no regeneration is approved for that review intake.

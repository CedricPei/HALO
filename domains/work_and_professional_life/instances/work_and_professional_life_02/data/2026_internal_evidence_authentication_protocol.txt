Internal Evidence Review Authentication Protocol
Effective: 2026-01-01

An LMS-linked attendance roster may remain unsigned for ordinary retention. If an unsigned roster is named in an approved 2026 internal evidence review sample, it requires a contemporaneous electronic attestation from the instructor assigned in the LMS before evidence upload. Training Attest enables a session only after its sample identifier is approved. The workforce training coordinator may send the standard Training Attest link and session identifier directly to the assigned instructor. Substitute signers are not accepted. If instructor access has ended, only the safety manager may document an exception; the coordinator may not authenticate attendance or make certification findings.

AB-7 Recurrent Scan Observation Guidance
Effective: 2026-04-01

When two independent training observations within 14 calendar days document the same unprompted mirror-rescan omission, and neither observation involved an incident or an incomplete exercise, the workforce training coordinator may arrange one preventive 30-minute AB-7 coaching session before the employee's next articulated-bus service.

Before arranging coaching, the coordinator must confirm in qualification_records that the employee's AB-DRIVE qualification is active. An active record supports coaching without a certification referral; a non-active record must instead be referred to a safety manager. The coordinator may reserve an available no-cost slot by replying to the designated instructor with the employee ID, observation dates, qualification code, next assignment, and offered slot. The reservation does not suspend qualification, alter dispatch, impose discipline, or constitute a certification decision. Coaching must be completed before pull-out to serve as prevention for that assignment.

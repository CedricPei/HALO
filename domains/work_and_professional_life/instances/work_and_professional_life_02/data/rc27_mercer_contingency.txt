RC-27 Mercer Underpass Field Contingency
Approved: 2026-01-09
Session: RC27-2026-01-13

This contingency applies only when the south approach to Mercer Underpass is closed after the trainee has completed the depot-departure and controlled-stop segments. The assigned instructor, Nia Calder, may preserve those completed segments and continue the same attempt by using the controlled U-turn gate, proceeding east on Vale Avenue, north on Eighth Street, and rejoining RC-27 at checkpoint C4. Street control release code: RTC-27-S2.

A pause of no more than 25 minutes preserves the completed segments. The workforce training coordinator may relay this approved route and release code to the assigned instructor. The instructor remains responsible for safe vehicle operation and assessment decisions; the coordinator may not direct vehicle operation or make a certification finding.

BD-6 BUS-218 Packet Control Note
Approved: 2026-06-24

Following interlock retrofit IR-218, both BD-6 Revision 2 and Revision 3 remain approved instructional material; use of Revision 2 does not invalidate a session or create a certification exception. Revision 2 is configuration-generic, however, and omits the post-release indicator check used on BUS-218. Repeated practice from that sequence can reinforce leaving the emergency-door override engaged during a later recovery. Revision 3 is the recommended configuration-matched package for BUS-218 and adds the indicator check without changing learning objectives or assessment standards.

When a BD-6 session is confirmed on BUS-218 and Central Training Print Services reports a Revision 2 matched batch still awaiting physical release, the workforce training coordinator may request one no-cost source correction in the existing print coordination channel. The request must identify the session ID, course code BD-6, vehicle BUS-218, print job ID, current Revision 2, and approved replacement source CURR-BD6-R3. Print Services will replace the trainee cards and instructor guide together.

The coordinator may not edit either revision, make a certification finding, or direct vehicle operation. After physical batch release, a coordinator-level digital or partial substitution is not approved because instructor checkpoints and trainee cards must remain matched; any later change to delivery requires a safety-manager decision.

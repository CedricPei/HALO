LM-4627 standing delivery checks
Courier pouch: the signed readme, query appendix and completed checklist are required physical contents. The spare generic 4 mm linguist handbook is optional; the identical office handbook is already available digitally and can be retained at the desk. Never remove required client documents, fold originals, or force a pouch beyond its marked thickness. The pack-station operator must remeasure before sealing.
Compact cards: a one-line 320 px action label must retain both its action and destination. A wrapped label is not automatically repaired by shortening it: request a qualified Spanish/Tagalog review using only the string key, language and layout dimensions, then route any wording recommendation through the account lead. Do not override approved translations or include patient screenshots.
Client carousel exports: the client asset coordinator can provide a de-identified visual-QA set that masks patient identity and visit details while preserving all interface labels and layout. Local translators must not distribute live patient panels or guess which clinical values can be retained.
